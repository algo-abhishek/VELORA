Our Story

About Velora

Velora started as a small project with one simple idea: buying shoes online should feel as easy as trying them on in a store. What began as a demo has grown into a full concept store covering footwear for men, women and kids alike.

What We Offer

From everyday running shoes to formal wear, sneakers and sports shoes, we bring together a range of styles under one roof, with clear pricing in Rupees, honest reviews and a simple checkout experience.

Our Promise

Free shipping over ₹2,999, easy 30-day returns and secure checkout, because comfort should extend beyond just the shoe.
