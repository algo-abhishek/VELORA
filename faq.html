Frequently Asked Questions

How do I choose the right shoe size?

Check our Size Guide on the Policies page, it maps UK sizes to approximate foot length in cm so you can measure at home before ordering.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Cash on Delivery, UPI, all major credit/debit cards, and net banking.

How long does delivery take?

Most orders are delivered within 3-7 business days depending on your location.

Can I return or exchange a product?

Yes, unused items in original packaging can be returned or exchanged within 30 days of delivery.

How do I track my order?

Go to My Account → My Orders to see the live status of every order you have placed.

Do you offer Cash on Delivery everywhere?

COD is available across most pincodes in India, availability is confirmed automatically at checkout.
